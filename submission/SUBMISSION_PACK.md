## 3. Copy blocks

### Title, year, medium (as one line)

Liminal Engine, 2026. Interactive installation: camera, projection, generative sound. Dimensions variable.

### One line

The mirror only becomes still when you do.

### Short description (50 words)

A sphere of liquid metal that only settles, and only turns to gold, when the visitor is still. A camera measures movement in the room without recording it. At the end the sphere becomes a mirror. Visitors can leave a sentence, which is engraved on the outer ring for the next person.

### Artist's statement (250 words)

Use the statement in `CURATORIAL_STATEMENT.md`, section "Artist's statement". It is exactly 250 words.

### Technical requirements and dimensions (short form, for forms with a small box)

Scalable to two formats: (1) Dark room (3 × 3 m min) with a bench facing a 1080p/4K projection or 65"+ display, stereo speakers + sub, and a USB webcam; or (2) Sculptural Plinth Edition using a 65–100 cm 3D LED holographic fan (`?fan=1`) requiring 1 × 1 m floor space and no wall. Hands-free operation: sitting still before the camera for 3 seconds starts the 90-second work automatically; visitors can speak or type a reflection at the end. Runs offline on any PC/Mac in Chrome kiosk mode. No camera images are stored or transmitted. Full rider: `INSTALLATION_SPECIFICATIONS.md`.

### Short biography (fill in; keep to 80 to 100 words, third person)

> Dante Harker is an artist working with code, sound and light. [One sentence on where you are based and what you do day to day.] [One sentence on what your work is about, in plain words, for example: his work asks what screens could do for us if they stopped asking things of us.] [One sentence on any previous shows, publications or the fact that this is a first submission, which is fine to say.] Liminal Engine (2026) is his first interactive installation.
